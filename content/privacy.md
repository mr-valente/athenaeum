# Privacy

Google sign-in is optional. I use your Google account ID, name, and email to
recognize your account and save activity across apps and devices.

Saved activity includes answers, scores, and classroom settings. Teachers can
see results in their classrooms; other participants may see display names and
scores on leaderboards. Your Google email is not shown there.

I use cookies to keep you signed in and remember classroom participation. Guest activity
stays separate unless you choose to save it to your account.

I store data on my server and in encrypted backups. I use it to run the apps
and provide your history. I don't sell it or use it for advertising.

I keep records until removed. You can download your history from
[your account](/account/). For corrections or deletion, contact me using
the details on [my GitHub profile](https://github.com/mr-valente).
Backup copies expire according to the backup retention schedule.
