# Privacy

Google sign-in is optional. We use your Google account ID, name, and email to
recognize your account and save activity across apps and devices.

Saved activity includes answers, scores, and classroom settings. Teachers can
see results in their classrooms; other participants may see display names and
scores on leaderboards. Your Google email is not shown there.

Cookies keep you signed in and remember classroom participation. Guest activity
stays separate unless you choose to save it to your account.

Data is stored on our server and in encrypted backups. It is used to run the
apps and provide your history, not for advertising or sale.

Records are kept until removed. You can download your history from
[your account](/account/). For corrections or deletion, contact Mr. Valente using
the details on [his GitHub profile](https://github.com/mr-valente).
Backup copies expire according to the backup retention schedule.
